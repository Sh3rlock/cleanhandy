Quotes

A quote request is not a booking. You tell us the service, the address, the size band, access, and anything the crew needs to know. Staff then send a written price based on that description, including any discount and sales tax shown on the quote.

Time guides on the site, such as cleaner count and hours for a size band, are planning figures. They are not a flat rate and they are not a promise that your home will take that long.

You can accept the written quote from the email link or from your account, or you can decline it. Declining before you pay does not create a charge. Acceptance means you want the work at the quoted price. The preferred date is held when we receive the deposit or the full payment described below. If that payment does not arrive, staff may release the date.

Price, tax, and payment

The price on the written quote is the price for the scope on that quote. Sales tax, when it applies, is calculated on the discounted subtotal and shown as its own line. A discount applies only when it appears on the quote or is accepted on the payment page.

You may pay a 50% deposit or the full amount. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number. The remaining balance, if you paid the deposit, is due on the day of the job unless the quote says otherwise. Staff can issue an invoice against an accepted job. An invoice is a record of what was billed.

If the crew arrives and the space is blocked, without the water or power the service needs, or materially different from the request, the estimate can be revised before work continues, or the visit can be stopped. Extra rooms, heavier carries, stairs that were not described, construction debris, or a home that is still full of furniture are examples of a material difference.

Scheduling and access

Cleaning

Move-out, move-in, apartment move-out, rental turnover, and deep cleaning follow the checklist on the written quote. The usual scope is kitchens, bathrooms, floors, interior cabinets when they are empty, appliance exteriors, interior glass, and dust on baseboards and doors. Inside the fridge, oven, or microwave is included when those appliances are empty and the quote lists them.

Move-in cleaning is meant for an empty or nearly empty home, before furniture arrives. Move-out and turnover cleaning work best after the furniture is out. Furniture left in the rooms can change the time and the price.

A clean does not decide a security deposit, a lease dispute, or whether a landlord will accept the unit. Those decisions stay with the landlord or manager. If the crew finds damage, leftover furniture, or missing utilities, they can note it and stop or reprice the visit.

Post-renovation cleaning is for dust and debris after paint, plaster, or light construction, once the trades are finished. It does not include hauling construction waste unless the quote says so. If the site is not ready, the quoted time does not apply.

Post-event cleaning covers the rooms that were used: surfaces, floors, baths, and the kitchen. Trash is removed when it is already collected or listed on the quote.

If a listed item on the cleaning scope was missed, email or call within 48 hours and we will come back to correct that item. This is a return visit for the missed scope, not a promise about a landlord’s deposit.

Commercial and recurring work

Office, retail, property-management, and other commercial cleaning are quoted from the rooms, restrooms, days, and access you describe. The residential time guide does not set the commercial price. After-hours entry, alarms, and keys belong in the request. Restroom products are yours to supply unless the quote says we bring them.

A recurring schedule is the checklist on the days the quote names. Changes to days, rooms, or access should be in writing. Either of us can end a recurring schedule with at least 7 days’ notice before the next visit. A single visit cancelled inside 48 hours of its start can still be charged.

Property-manager accounts can keep more than one address. Each unit is quoted on its own size and condition. Invoices are issued per job.

Assembly, mounting, and painting

Assembly covers the pieces listed on the quote. Mounting covers the items listed, after you confirm the building allows them on that wall. Hardware is chosen for the wall when it can be confirmed on site. Plaster, brick, and drywall are not the same job, and a wall type you did not describe can change the quote.

Painting covers the rooms named on the quote, with basic protection of floors and fixtures in those rooms. Ceilings, color changes, and repairs are included only when the quote lists them. Leave dry time before furniture comes back or before a deep clean. Paint is supplied as the quote states.
